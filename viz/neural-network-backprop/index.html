Multilayer Feedforward Neural Networks

The network

A fully connected (dense) network of L layers. Every neuron in layer l connects to every neuron in layer l+1. The number inside each neuron is its value; the green b units are bias neurons (one per layer). Click any neuron to reveal the weights w and bias b on its incoming connections — the full calculation appears in the panel just below the diagram.

When you click a neuron, its incoming weights are coloured: −1 +1 (thicker = larger magnitude)

What each neuron computes

Activation of neuron i in layer l+1
$$ x_{i,p}^{\,l+1} = \sigma\!\left(\xi_{i,p}^{\,l+1}\right) $$

The neuron's output: run its weighted input ξ through the activation function σ.

Weighted input ("pre-activation")
$$ \xi_{i,p}^{\,l+1} = \sum_{j=1}^{N_l} w_{ij}^{\,l+1}\, x_{j,p}^{\,l} + b_i^{\,l+1} $$

Sum over every neuron j in the previous layer of (its output × the connecting weight), plus the neuron's own bias b.

Note: in this interactive diagram the activation σ is applied at the output layer; the hidden layers display their raw weighted sum ξ so you can follow the arithmetic. Every non-input neuron also carries a bias term, which is why the trainable-parameter count above includes both weights and biases.

Symbol legend

  • \(L\) — total number of layers (so \(L-1\) hidden layers).
  • \(N_l\) — number of neurons in layer \(l\).
  • \(P\) — number of input patterns; \(p\) indexes a single pattern.
  • \(x_{j,p}^{\,l}\) — output (activation) of neuron \(j\) in layer \(l\) for pattern \(p\).
  • \(w_{ij}^{\,l+1}\) — weight from neuron \(j\) (layer \(l\)) to neuron \(i\) (layer \(l+1\)).
  • \(b_i^{\,l+1}\) — bias added to neuron \(i\) in layer \(l+1\).
  • \(\xi_{i,p}^{\,l+1}\) — weighted sum reaching neuron \(i\) before activation.
  • \(\sigma(\cdot)\) — the activation function (e.g. sigmoid).
